Welcome to the Department of CSE - Cyber Security

CSE - Cyber Security

Welcome to our Computer Science and Engineering - Cyber Security department. Our department focuses on computer security, programming, networking and protecting digital information.

Cyber Security is an important field because computers and the internet are used in almost every part of our daily life.

About Our Department

The CSE - Cyber Security department helps students understand how computers, networks and applications can be protected from different security threats.

Students learn programming languages, web technologies, networking, databases and security concepts. We also learn how to identify security problems and find suitable solutions.

Cyber Security is not only about computers. It is also about protecting people, organizations and information.

How Cyber Security is Helpful in the Future

In the future, more activities will depend on computers, mobile phones, cloud services and the internet. Because of this, cyber security will become even more important.

Cyber security professionals will help protect personal information, banking systems, companies, hospitals and government organizations. They will also work with new technologies such as artificial intelligence, cloud computing and the Internet of Things.

Cyber Security provides many career opportunities for students who are interested in technology and problem solving.
