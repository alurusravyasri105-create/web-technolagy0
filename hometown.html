Welcome to Tirupati - My Hometown!

Introduction

Tirupati is a major city located in the Tirupati district of Andhra Pradesh, India. It sits right at the bottom of the holy Seshachalam Hills. People call it the Spiritual Capital of Andhra Pradesh because it is deeply rooted in ancient culture, history, and religious traditions.


The Holy Tirumala Temple

My hometown is globally famous for the Sri Venkateswara Temple, which sits high up on the Tirumala hills. The temple has seven peaks, representing the seven hoods of Adisesha. Millions of devotees walk up the steps or take buses up the hills every single year to catch a glimpse of Lord Venkateswara, also lovingly called Balaji or Govinda.

Famous Local Attractions

  • Sri Govindaraja Swamy Temple: A very old temple located right in the heart of Tirupati city with a massive, beautiful gateway tower (Gopuram).
  • Kapila Theertham: A peaceful temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, located at the base of the Tirumala hills right next to a stunning natural waterfall.
  • Chandragiri Fort: A historical fort located just outside the city, which was the final capital of the famous Vijayanagara Empire.
  • Sri Venkateswara Zoological Park: One of the largest zoos in Asia, where we can see many animals living in wide open spaces.

Delicious Tirupati Food

No report on Tirupati is complete without mentioning the world-famous Tirupati Laddu! It is given as sacred 'prasadam' at the temple. It is incredibly sweet and made with pure ghee, sugar, cardamom, and nuts. In the city, we also enjoy traditional South Indian foods like crispy Dosa, soft Idli, and spicy Andhra meals served on banana leaves.

An Educational Hub

Tirupati is not just about temples! It is also known as a great place for students to study. My city is home to several top universities, including Sri Venkateswara University (SVU), Sri Padmavati Mahila Visvavidyalayam (a university just for women), and premier national institutions like IIT Tirupati and IISER Tirupati.
