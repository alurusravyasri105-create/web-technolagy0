VOL. I

The Origin Story!

Why did we build this engine?

Before this initiative, information was trapped inside separate silos! Silos of lost emails, untracked group chats, and old cork bulletin boards hidden away in dusty corridors.

We created **College Connect** to smash these barriers. We saw students missing out on cross-department hackathons, crucial notes, and vital peer support networks simply due to fractured communication channels.

WHY?

Mission Accomplished!

How it rescued the student body!

The impact was seismic! By consolidating resources, students achieved remarkable new heights:

  • Resource Sharing: Thousands of verified lecture logs and exam blueprints exchanged safely.
  • Dynamic Synergy: Instant formation of inter-disciplinary teams for major engineering and art events.
  • Mentorship Loops: Seniors instantly counseling juniors without structural boundaries.
